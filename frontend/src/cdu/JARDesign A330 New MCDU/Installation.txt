Since then, I find JARDesign A330's pop-up MCDU texture not realistic. From the color of the MCDU to its texture itself.
So I made a texture replacement for the pop-up MCDU of the JARDesign A330 to make it look realistic (like ToLiss' MCDU) 
to add to the overall satisfaction while flying the aircraft. 

Installation: (Backup original files first in case you want to revert back.)

- Just put the files inside the MCDU folder to: X-Plane 11/Aircraft/Laminar Research/JARDesign A330/plugins/fmgs/MCDU
- Then replace all the files inside JARDesign A330's MCDU folder.



If you want to make your JARDesign A330 much more realistic, I highly recommend to download these retextures from the provided links below.

• JARDesign A330 Cockpit Retextured by Verticalsim: https://forums.x-plane.org/index.php?/files/file/64150-jardesign-a330-cockpit-rextured/

• JARDesign A330 (PFD, ND, TARA) Fix by GA142: https://forums.x-plane.org/index.php?/files/file/86159-jardesign-a330-jd330-pfd-nd-tara-fix/z
